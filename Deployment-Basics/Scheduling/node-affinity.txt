yaml
affinity:

Common mistakes to look for:

Gt/Lt values must be quoted strings ("4"), with exactly one value.
DoesNotExist takes no values field.
If each condition has its own - matchExpressions: dash, it becomes OR, and the pod could land on either node.

Question 2: AND vs OR (nodeSelectorTerms vs matchExpressions)

Setup:

bash
k create ns aff2

Tasks:

Create Deployment or-app (nginx, 4 replicas) in aff2 that may run on a node if either of these is true:

Now edit the Deployment so that env=prod or disk=ssd alone is enough on node01, in addition to zone=b. Verify that node01 is eligible again after another scale cycle.
In one sentence, state which YAML level means AND and which means OR.
Correct YAML for your requirement
Replace your affinity section with:


yaml
      affinity:
        nodeAffinity:
          requiredDuringSchedulingIgnoredDuringExecution:
            nodeSelectorTerms:
            - matchExpressions:
              - key: zone
                operator: In
                values:
                - b
            - matchExpressions:
              - key: env
                operator: In
                values:
                - prod
              - key: disk
                operator: In
                values:
                - ssd
Understand the difference
Your requirement:


text
zone=b OR (env=prod AND disk=ssd)
The YAML represents it like this:


text
nodeSelectorTerms:
  - term 1:
      zone=b

  - term 2:
      env=prod
      disk=ssd
First - creates the first term.

Second - creates the second term.

Both expressions inside the second term must match.

Validate and apply

bash
k apply --dry-run=server -f orapp.yaml
k apply -f orapp.yaml
CKA exam golden rule: nodeSelectorTerms is a list of OR conditions, and matchExpressions is a list of AND conditions.

Hints without spoilers

k explain pod.spec.affinity.nodeAffinity --recursive | less shows the structure. In the docs, search “Assign Pods to Nodes” and copy the requiredDuringScheduling... example.
Gt and Lt take exactly one value, written as a string of an integer (["4"]). Exists and DoesNotExist take no values.
Dashes matter: each - matchExpressions under nodeSelectorTerms is a separate term (OR). Several - key: entries inside one matchExpressions are ANDed.
weight is 1 to 100, and each preferred entry has weight plus a preference block (not nodeSelectorTerms).
kubectl rollout restart or scaling to 0 and back lets you re-run scheduling for a Deployment.
